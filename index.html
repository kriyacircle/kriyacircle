Our Purpose

Healing the Mind,
One Breath at a Time

Kriya Circle is a nonprofit organization founded on the belief that ancient Kriya practices carry profound healing potential for the modern world. Rooted in the authentic teachings of His Holiness Swami Advaitananda Giri, we bring these powerful techniques to communities across the United States — freely and accessibly.

Mental health challenges touch every community, every household. Anxiety, stress, and emotional suffering do not discriminate by race, gender, age, or background. Neither do we. Kriya Circle exists to offer everyone a proven, time-honored path toward inner peace and resilience.

Our Teacher

His Holiness Swami
Advaitananda Giri

His Holiness Swami Advaitananda Giri is a revered spiritual master whose life's work has been devoted to making the transformative teachings of India's ancient meditative traditions accessible to the modern world.

Drawing from the vast ocean of Vedic and Tantric wisdom — including the Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra's 112 methods of meditation and the teachings of the Bhagavad Gita — Swamiji has developed practical, accessible Kriya techniques that address the mental health and spiritual needs of people from all walks of life.

His work spans international mental health conferences in cities like Santa Monica, a World Peace Tour, the Meditation Foundation at meditation.ca, and a lifelong mission of holistic healing for humanity. The Kriyas he teaches are living transmissions from an authentic, unbroken lineage.

What We Teach

The Kriyas

Each Kriya is a complete technology of transformation — a carefully structured practice that purifies the nervous system, quiets the mind, and opens the heart to its natural state of peace.

🌬️

mKriya

Technique for Inner Peace

A foundational Kriya practice designed for the modern mind. mKriya works directly with the breath and awareness to dissolve stress, calm the nervous system, and cultivate a stable sense of inner peace. Available in 1-hour and 3-hour 45-minute deep-dive formats.

Anxiety Relief Stress Reduction Inner Peace
☀️

Mahamrityunjay Kriya

Victory Over Suffering

Rooted in one of the most ancient Vedic mantras, this profound Kriya invokes the healing energy of the universe. Practiced for centuries to overcome fear, trauma, and deep-seated suffering, it works at the subtlest levels of consciousness to restore wholeness.

Deep Healing Trauma Recovery Vitality
🔔

Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra

112 Paths to Stillness

An ancient text containing 112 distinct meditation techniques. These practices use sound, breath, visualization, and awareness to dissolve the boundary between the individual mind and universal consciousness. Accessible to beginners, profound for advanced practitioners.

Consciousness Self-Discovery Meditation
🌙

Yoga Nidra

The Sleep of the Yogis

Yoga Nidra guides practitioners into the hypnagogic state between waking and sleep — the deepest level of conscious rest. It rewires the nervous system, releases stored tension, and creates profound mental and emotional restoration. One hour equals four hours of sleep.

Sleep Quality Deep Rest Nervous System
🌀

Pranayama & Breathwork

The Science of Vital Force

Pranayama — the ancient science of breath — gives us direct access to the autonomic nervous system. These structured breathing practices reduce cortisol, activate the parasympathetic response, and create immediate, measurable relief from anxiety and stress.

Anxiety Breath Nervous System
🎵

Sound Healing

Vibrational Medicine

Ancient traditions understood that sound is medicine. Through mantra, singing bowls, and intentional vibrational practices, sound healing entrains the brain into healing states, dissolves mental chatter, and awakens a deep sense of presence and peace.

Sound Therapy Mantra Well-Being

Why Kriyas Work

Science Meets Ancient Wisdom

The Kriyas taught at Kriya Circle are experiential practices with documented effects on the mind, nervous system, and overall well-being — validated by both ancient tradition and modern science.

01

Reduces Anxiety & Stress

Kriya practices directly regulate the autonomic nervous system, shifting the body from the fight-or-flight response to the rest-and-repair state. Studies on breathwork and meditation show significant reductions in anxiety markers within weeks of regular practice.

02

Improves Emotional Resilience

Regular practice builds the capacity to observe emotions without being overwhelmed by them. Practitioners report greater equanimity, reduced emotional reactivity, and a stable sense of inner groundedness even during difficult circumstances.

03

Enhances Mental Clarity

Kriya practices clear the mental fog caused by chronic stress and anxiety. Practitioners consistently report sharper focus, improved decision-making, greater creativity, and a sense of mental spaciousness that supports daily functioning.

04

Supports Better Sleep

Practices like Yoga Nidra directly address the hyperarousal that disrupts sleep. By calming the nervous system and releasing stored tension, these techniques help restore natural, restorative sleep patterns that medication alone cannot achieve.

05

Trauma-Informed Healing

Many Kriyas work somatically — through the body — making them uniquely effective for trauma recovery. They allow the nervous system to process and release stored trauma gently, complementing and enhancing other therapeutic approaches.

06

Builds Community & Belonging

Mental health thrives in connection. Kriya Circle cultivates a supportive, inclusive community where every person is welcomed and valued. The shared experience of practice creates bonds of understanding that transcend cultural and social divides.
